02

Cost curves · scenario assumptions

There is no supplied benchmark or procurement dataset for either setup. Initial values (€250 / 15 W and €750 / 130 W) are illustrative inputs chosen for this interface. They are not gAIn performance claims, RTX 3050 specifications, current market prices, or evidence of validated savings. Edit the assumptions to enter your own data.

Each curve is a linear replication model: every hospital receives the same number of setups. In electricity mode, energy cost is power in kW × operating hours × tariff. Hardware is purchased once. No economies of scale, price escalation or discounting are assumed. All prices are EUR; tax treatment follows the entered prices.

04

Two-screen connection

Same-browser tabs sync locally. Separate devices use a WebRTC data connection, with the public PeerJS service for connection signalling. Internet access is required for pairing; some institutional networks block peer connections. Only scenario settings are exchanged. No patient data is used.
